About us H&M Group is a global fashion and design company, with over 4,000 stores in more than 80 markets and online sales in over 60 markets. All our brands and ventures share the same passion for making fashion and design accessible to everyone. Each brand has its own unique identity, and together they complement each other and strengthen H&M Group.
