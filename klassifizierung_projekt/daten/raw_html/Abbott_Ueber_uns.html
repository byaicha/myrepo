What is Abbott and what do they do?

Abbott is a global healthcare leader with a nearly 140-year history of creating breakthrough products. Today, we serve more than 2 billion people around the globe. What began as a small pharmacy in Chicago has grown into one of the most respected healthcare companies in the world. Our portfolio now spans Cardiovascular, Diabetes, Diagnostics, Pain and Movement, Nutrition, Medicines and Biowearables.

Our mission is to help people live fuller lives through better health as we deliver innovative solutions across the entire healthcare spectrum. This dedication to innovation is reflected in our leadership, in our consistent investment in research and development, and in our commitment to providing access to our life-changing technologies worldwide.

 Who is the CEO of Abbott?

Abbott’s Chairman and CEO is Robert B. Ford, who assumed the role of Chairman in December 2021, having been appointed President and Chief Executive Officer in March 2020.

 Where is Abbott headquarters?

Abbott's corporate headquarters is in Lake County, Illinois, at Abbott Park, a suburb 40 miles north of Chicago, IL, U.S.
